Why a Rebuild

Your current site has a strong foundation — but critical blockers

The existing site scores 92/100 on technical performance and already earns ~148 visits a month. But it's being held back by fixable problems that are costing you leads every single day.

❌
New York Contact Block Live on Homepage
The site is publicly showing "123 Fifth Avenue, New York, USA" as a contact address. Google can't confirm you're a Mackay business, destroying local trust and map-pack rankings.
Critical
❌
"Lawn Mowing Mackay" Mapped to Wrong Page
Your #1 keyword (260 searches/month) is currently ranking #70 — and pointing to the gardening page instead of the lawn mowing page. It's invisible to anyone searching it.

❌

Critical
⚠️
